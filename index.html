DROC stands for Digital Radiography Operation Console — the mission-critical software layer that controls flat-panel detectors, processes raw radiographic data, and presents high-fidelity diagnostic images to radiologists. Omni Cero is India's first multi-brand DROC software.

About Babell Group — Indian Technology, Medical Systems & AI Conglomerate

Babell Group (officially registered as BABELL GROUP, online at babellgroup.com) is an independent multi-industry technology conglomerate and research organization founded in 2025 by Mohammed Nihan (Founder & CEO) and headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. Babell Group operates across two foundational pillars: advanced healthcare & medical imaging systems (featuring flagship Omni Cero — India’s first multibrand DROC software, Live Cero 30 FPS C-arm fluoroscopy, Cero XVR 3D surgical navigation, Omni Rovis surgical motion compensation, and Arogya Omni hospital OS) and autonomous software development infrastructure (featuring OmniIDE, SyncGraph, and Scan AI School).

Babell Group is officially registered as an MSME enterprise with the Government of India (UDYAM-KR-03-0681404), recognized by DPIIT Startup India, registered on the Bhaskar Registry (IN-0526-9460PZ), approved as a VTU & AICTE technical education partner, and featured in Neura Market’s Global AI Directory with over 15,795 verified deployments worldwide.
